These are working documents, not marking checklists. Copy the relevant one,
fill it in as you go, and submit it as part of the assessment that calls for
it. Each is also available as a plain-text file you can download and edit
directly.

## Strategy memo

Used before the Alignment Lab's pre-meeting contact window opens.

```text
STRATEGY MEMO — [your role]

My responsibilities:
My evidence:
What I don't know:
Who I plan to contact, and why:
Ethical boundaries I won't cross:
```

[Download `strategy-memo.txt`](/templates/strategy-memo.txt)

## Negotiation log

A running record of pre-meeting contact — used or explicitly declined.

```text
NEGOTIATION LOG — [your role]

One entry per contact — self-initiated, or a reply to someone else's
request. You may make at most two self-initiated contacts by default;
replies you make to requests from others are not capped, and belong in this
log too.

Contact — [who] — [when] — [self-initiated / reply to their request]:
Purpose:
New information exchanged:
Any commitment made, by either side:
Did either person's judgment change, and how:

(Copy the block above for every contact you make or receive. If you chose
not to make a self-initiated contact at all: say so, and why.)
```

[Download `negotiation-log.txt`](/templates/negotiation-log.txt)

## Agenda

Used for any exercise that asks you to write or rewrite a meeting agenda.

```text
AGENDA — [meeting name] — [date]

Item: [title — does it name an open question or a foregone conclusion?]
Purpose: [what the room is actually being asked to do]
Time allocated: [minutes, and what that signals]
```

[Download `agenda.txt`](/templates/agenda.txt)

## Decision record

The one document the Alignment Lab group produces together.

```text
DECISION RECORD — [scenario name] — [date]

The decision (or a reasoned deferral):
Evidence relied on:
Unverified assumptions:
Who agreed:
Who dissented, and on what grounds (including conditional or partial support):
Action owner(s) — who carries out what follows from this:
Final decision authority — who actually had the authority to make this call:
What would reopen this record:
```

[Download `decision-record.txt`](/templates/decision-record.txt)

## Decision autopsy

The structure the [Decision Autopsy](/assessments/decision-autopsy/)
assessment asks you to follow.

```text
DECISION AUTOPSY — [decision] — [your name]

The decision, in one sentence, with a link to where it was announced:
The timeline (every dated document, in order, with a one-line note):
The seam, or why there isn't one (a specific point where the outcome looks
settled before the forum that announced it, with an alternative explanation
you considered and ruled out — or a reasoned case that the record shows a
genuinely open or indeterminate process):
What you cannot show (the gap between evidence and suspicion):
```

[Download `decision-autopsy.txt`](/templates/decision-autopsy.txt)

## Decision system redesign

The structure the
[Decision System Redesign](/assessments/decision-system-redesign/)
assessment asks you to follow.

```text
DECISION SYSTEM REDESIGN — [process being redesigned]

Who currently decides, and how:
What is wrong with it today, with evidence (hard to challenge, trace, or
attribute — not "it involves talking before the meeting"):
The replacement: who is invited, when, on what information, with what
record, and what channel (if any) still exists for informal pre-meeting
coordination:
Who loses influence under the new design:
Tested against one concrete scenario — what would the new design actually
produce, step by step:
```

[Download `redesign.txt`](/templates/redesign.txt)
