These are working documents, not marking checklists. Copy the relevant one,
fill it in as you go, and submit it as part of the assessment that calls for
it. Each is also available as a plain-text file you can download and edit
directly.

## Strategy memo

Used before the Alignment Lab's pre-meeting contact window opens.

```text
STRATEGY MEMO — [your role]

What I want:
What I can concede, and in what order:
What I will not concede, under any circumstance:
Who I expect to agree with me, and why:
Who I expect to oppose me, and why:
```

[Download `strategy-memo.txt`](/templates/strategy-memo.txt)

## Negotiation log

A running record of pre-meeting contact — used or explicitly declined.

```text
NEGOTIATION LOG — [your role]

Contact 1: [who] — [when] — [what you learned or offered]
Contact 2: [who] — [when] — [what you learned or offered]
(If you chose not to make contact: say so, and why.)
```

[Download `negotiation-log.txt`](/templates/negotiation-log.txt)

## Agenda

Used for any exercise that asks you to write or rewrite a meeting agenda.

```text
AGENDA — [meeting name] — [date]

Item: [title — does it name an open question or a foregone conclusion?]
Purpose: [what the room is actually being asked to do]
Time allocated: [minutes, and what that signals]
```

[Download `agenda.txt`](/templates/agenda.txt)

## Decision record

The one document the Alignment Lab group produces together.

```text
DECISION RECORD — [scenario name] — [date]

The decision:
Who agreed:
Who dissented, and on what grounds:
What information arrived too late to change the outcome, if any:
Who signs off on this record:
```

[Download `decision-record.txt`](/templates/decision-record.txt)

## Decision autopsy

The structure the [Decision Autopsy](/assessments/decision-autopsy/)
assessment asks you to follow.

```text
DECISION AUTOPSY — [decision] — [your name]

The decision, in one sentence, with a link to where it was announced:
The timeline (every dated document, in order, with a one-line note):
The seam (where the outcome looks settled before the forum that announced it):
What you cannot show (the gap between evidence and suspicion):
```

[Download `decision-autopsy.txt`](/templates/decision-autopsy.txt)

## Decision system redesign

The structure the
[Decision System Redesign](/assessments/decision-system-redesign/)
assessment asks you to follow.

```text
DECISION SYSTEM REDESIGN — [process being redesigned]

Who currently decides, and how:
What is wrong with it today, with evidence:
The replacement: who is invited, when, on what information, with what record:
Who loses influence under the new design:
```

[Download `redesign.txt`](/templates/redesign.txt)
