## Outline

- why this distinction, not some other, is the course's method: every later
  week — invitations, agendas, silence, minutes — is this same move applied to
  a different kind of document
- what makes an inference *reasonable* rather than merely *plausible*: it has
  to survive being stated next to the document it is drawn from
- the debrief: the agreed classification for the full ten-entry case
  timeline (below)
- what the course is not claiming: that every organisation hides its real
  decisions before the formal meeting, or that pre-meeting coordination is
  automatically improper — sometimes it produces a better-informed room,
  sometimes it produces a room with nothing left to decide, and the public
  record alone often cannot tell you which
- reading before Week 2: the Steering Committee's invitation list for the
  2027-03-08 meeting (distributed in the Week 2 session)

## The debrief: the case timeline, classified

| Date | Entry | Kind | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2027-01-18 | Finance & Risk signs off the ClarityPulse budget | Fact | The memo states the amount and carries a signature |
| 2027-01-22 | Herrera meets ClarityPulse's sales lead | Fact | Calendar entry only proves the meeting happened, nothing said in it |
| 2027-01-29 | Draft plan names LSS as first rollout site | Fact | The document directly names the site — that part is a fact. Why LSS was picked is a separate, unanswered question |
| 2027-02-03 | LSS staff first hear "ClarityPulse" | Unknown | No dated document fixes when; informal staff recollection is not a documentary source |
| 2027-02-10 | Nandakumar's email names a go-live term | Fact | Time-stamped and forwarded; whether the term was newly proposed or already fixed is separate |
| 2027-02-24 | Okoro requests an information session | Fact | Dated email exists; whether it was granted is a later, separate fact |
| 2027-03-01 | Draft B frames Item 4 as open | Fact | The file's own metadata carries this date |
| 2027-03-03 | Draft A frames Item 4 as settled | Fact | This is the version actually sent |
| 2027-03-01 to 2027-03-03 | Someone decided the item should read as settled | Inference | No document names who or why, and no single document carries this claim's date — it takes the gap's date range rather than a false-precise day. See the worked example on the [session page](/sessions/01-getting-started/) for the full reasoning, including the equally reasonable alternative reading |
| 2027-03-08 | Steering Committee confirms the timeline | Fact | Minutes are dated and attributed |

Two entries are marked "fact" that a first read often calls "inference" —
2027-02-10 and 2027-02-24. The document being dated and attributed is what
makes the *entry itself* a fact; the *further claim* many readers add
("this proves the term was already decided," "this proves the request was
ignored") is the inference, and it is a different sentence from the one in
the table. Keeping those two sentences separate is the entire skill.
