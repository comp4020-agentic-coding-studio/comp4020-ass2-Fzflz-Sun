## Before the session

Bring a first draft of the process you intend to redesign for the
[Decision System Redesign](/assessments/decision-system-redesign/)
assessment. This is the last teaching session of the semester; the
assessment itself is not due until two weeks from today.

## In the session

Every week so far has been a reading skill: naming what a document shows,
what it omits, and what it argues for without saying so. This closing
session turns that reading skill outward, into a design constraint. A
redesign that only bans "talking before the meeting" fails immediately —
informal coordination has legitimate uses, and pretending otherwise
produces a process nobody actually follows. The session works through what
a redesign has to specify to be runnable: who is invited, on what
information, with what record, and what — if any — channel still exists for
informal pre-meeting conversation.

Groups peer-review one another's draft redesigns against a single test:
could a person who was not in this room run this process from the
document alone, and would it produce a traceable decision if they did?

## After the session

Submit your [Decision System Redesign](/assessments/decision-system-redesign/),
tested against at least one concrete scenario, two weeks from today. This
is the course's final assessed piece of work, and there is no session in
either of the two weeks before it is due.
