## Before the session

Read the [Alignment Lab](/alignment-lab/) page in full, including the
worked example. This week's session assigns roles and opens the pre-meeting
contact window for the assessment.

## In the session

A veto that is never used leaves no trace in a normal record — which means
a decision can look fully consensual on paper while one person's unspoken
capacity to block it shaped every option that was ever seriously put
forward. This week's short lecture segment separates "who signs the
document" from "who could have stopped this and didn't," using the
Engagement Signal Pilot's own sign-off chain as the worked case.

The bulk of the session hands out the four Alignment Lab role cards and
opens the strategy-memo and pre-meeting-contact stage of the assessment,
using the [templates](/templates/) and the contact rules described on the
Lab page.

## After the session

Everything else happens this week, not next. Submit your strategy memo
before making any contact. The pre-meeting contact window described on the
[Alignment Lab](/alignment-lab/) page runs 48–72 hours from today, subject
to the contact limit described there, and your group arranges and holds
its own decision meeting within that same window — it does not wait for
the next scheduled class. Week 10's session is the reveal, after your
group's decision record already exists.
